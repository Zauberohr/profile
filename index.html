Otto Solms is a man of many talents—architect by training, PR trainee by day, and undercover artist by night. Operating under the alias *Zauberohr*, he draws hilarious house illustrations that make people laugh (or at least smirk politely). He once photographed weddings but realized buildings don’t complain about bad angles. After surviving military service in reconnaissance, he now navigates the wild terrain of Berlin’s creative scene, currently tackling web development at Le Wagon. Armed with wordplay, wit, and a good dose of chaos, Otto’s mission is clear: **make people laugh, one absurd drawing at a time.
